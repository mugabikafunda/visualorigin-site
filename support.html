Visual Origin Support

Bold filters, a pro photo and video editor, carousels and a colorist, all on your iPhone and iPad.

Need help? Email visualorigin.app@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Where are my edits saved?

On your device, inside the app. Projects save automatically as you work. Visual Origin has no account and no cloud storage, so deleting the app also deletes its projects. Export anything you want to keep to your Photos library first (exporting needs Pro).

How do I use a look from Discover?

Tap a filter on the Discover stage or swipe across the photo, then tap Try this look. Choose Use on photo or Video to open it in the editor.

How do I compare before and after?

In Discover, tap Compare and drag across the photo. In the photo editor, press and hold the picture.

Captions aren't working

Automatic captions use speech recognition on your device. Make sure it's allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Speech Recognition, and that Siri & Dictation is set up for the language of your video.

The camera can't record Apple Log

Apple Log recording is part of Visual Origin Pro and works on iPhone 15 Pro and newer Pro models. On other iPhones, or without Pro, the camera records with a look applied instead.

What does Visual Origin Pro include?

You can try every tool for free. Pro saves and exports your photos and videos, unlocks Apple Log recording, and includes every future look. Choose yearly (with 7 days free for new subscribers), monthly, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. To cancel a subscription, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before it renews; you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Why are my screenshots black?

Without Pro, screenshots and screen recordings of Visual Origin show black, to protect Pro content. With Pro, you can capture the screen normally.

Saving to Photos fails

Allow Visual Origin to add to your library in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos, and make sure your device has free space.
