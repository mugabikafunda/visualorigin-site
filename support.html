Visual Origin Support

Bold filters, a pro photo and video editor, carousels and a colorist, all on your iPhone and iPad.

Need help? Email visualorigin.app@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Where are my edits saved?

On your device, inside the app. Projects save automatically as you work. Visual Origin has no account and no cloud storage, so deleting the app also deletes its projects. Export anything you want to keep to your Photos library first.

How do I use a look from Discover?

Tap a filter on the Discover stage or swipe across the photo, then tap Try this look. Choose Use on photo or Video to open it in the editor.

How do I compare before and after?

In Discover, tap Compare and drag across the photo. In the photo editor, press and hold the picture.

Captions aren't working

Automatic captions use speech recognition on your device. Make sure it's allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Speech Recognition, and that Siri & Dictation is set up for the language of your video.

The camera can't record Apple Log

Apple Log is available on iPhone 15 Pro and newer Pro models. On other iPhones the camera records with a look applied instead.

Saving to Photos fails

Allow Visual Origin to add to your library in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos, and make sure your device has free space.
