Privacy Policy

Visual Origin · Last updated 20 November 2026

Visual Origin is a photo and video editor that works entirely on your iPhone or iPad. We built it so that your photos, videos and edits stay yours.

The short version

Visual Origin does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends your photos, videos or edits anywhere. The only connections are to Apple, for things like Pro purchases and downloading languages for translation.

Your photos and videos

Photos and videos you choose to edit are copied into the app's own storage on your device, so your projects keep working. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting a project, or deleting the app, removes them.

Permissions

Visual Origin only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it, and you can change your choice at any time in the Settings app.

  • Camera: to take photos and record video in the app's camera.
  • Microphone: to record sound for camera videos and voiceovers.
  • Photos (add only): to save the photos and videos you export to your library. Photos you pick to edit are chosen with Apple's private picker, so the app never sees the rest of your library.
  • Speech recognition: to turn speech in your videos into captions. This runs on your device.

Purchases

Visual Origin Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; the app only receives Apple's confirmation that Pro is active, and never sees your payment details.

Settings and preferences

Choices such as your language, appearance and saved looks are stored on your device only.

Children

Visual Origin does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
